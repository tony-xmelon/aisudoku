These notes were wrong on your last seventeen builds.

The file that reaches you is written by hand, and nothing ever failed when it was
not rewritten - so every build since the second of September arrived carrying the
same paragraph about a corpus photograph. Each build now prints its own version and
the change that produced it at the top of these notes, generated rather than typed,
so that those two lines are true even when the rest has gone stale.

Here is what has actually reached you since then.

THE PAGE

An erased digit is no longer offered back to you as your own answer. On the page
you sent with the two-line candidate marks, one square held a rubbed-out digit with
two marks penciled over it, and the app read the ghost as something you had
written. Nothing about its shape says it is gone - it is digit-sized, and it sits
in the middle of the square, because it was a digit written there. What gives it
away is that it is faint AND that something darker is written in the same square,
which is what you leave behind when you rub a digit out and pencil over the ruins.
Either half alone would have cost real answers: the faintest answer in the corpus
is fainter than that erasure.

The candidate marks that wrap onto a second line no longer read as answers either.
Where a square holds four or more marks, the second line sits low and centred -
exactly where an answer goes, at nearly the size of one - and three of them on that
page were a lone 9 under a row of three digits.

The digit reader has been retrained on the two pages added since - the one with
the two-line candidate marks, and a sudoku photographed off a laptop screen,
which is the first thing in the collection that is not paper. Scored the honest
way, with each photograph read by a model that has never seen it, handwriting is
174 of 180 squares. That is the same as before within the run-to-run wobble, which
is what two more pages should do: nothing measurable yet, and a little more ground
under the next change.

THE CAMERA

The shutter no longer fires by itself on framing the app is about to refuse. It
allowed more tilt than the check that judges the photograph did, and did not look
at perspective at all, so it would tell you to hold still, take a picture, and then
refuse what it had just taken. Both now use the same limits, so what you are told
while aiming is what the photograph will be judged by.

Photographs refused because the digits could not be read are now kept, like the
ones refused for the grid. Only half of the refusals were being saved, and it was
the harder half that was thrown away - a grid the app found but could not read
depends on the page, the light and the lens together, and is the case worth having
the picture of.

THE APP

It works in landscape, and turning the phone no longer loses the puzzle you have
open. The grid moves to the left with everything else beside it, rather than
shrinking to a thumbnail on a screen twice as wide.

There is a Send diagnostics entry in the menu at the top right. It sends every
refused photograph at once with a short report - the version, the phone, and what
was refused - instead of one photograph at a time from the bottom of the puzzle
list. With nothing refused it sends the report alone, which is still an answer.

The shading that shows how far into a hint you are is now drawn inside the Hint
button rather than on its touch target, which is 8dp taller than the button you can
see. That is why it stood proud of the outline with its end cap outside the border.

Please keep sending photos of anything it gets wrong. Every fix above came from one
photograph that failed.
