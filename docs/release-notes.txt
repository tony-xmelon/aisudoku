Everything from the first round of feedback, plus a better recogniser.

The recogniser now works the way you suggested: find the printed digits first.
Printed digits share a font, a size and an ink, and a puzzle needs at least 17 of
them, so they are the one population that can be identified reliably. Once they are
known, anything bigger is handwriting and anything smaller is a pencil mark - so the
thresholds come from the photo in hand instead of a constant that had to suit every
photo at once. On a page covered in annotations even that is not enough, because 17
marks can look tighter than the print, so several candidates are tried and the solver
decides: the printed givens are by definition the set that forms a valid puzzle.

The annotated puzzle that failed now reads. All seven test photos read correctly.

Also in this build:
- Red cells now show the digit the app READ. Before, you saw the correct digit on
  your paper tinted red and reasonably concluded the solver was broken.
- Solve overlays the whole solution, not only the cells read as empty.
- Hint stops once the puzzle is complete.
- "Check the highlighted cells" clears when you have been through them, and each cell
  has a "Looks right" button to confirm a reading without changing it.
- Square viewfinder; shutter clear of the system buttons; it says "Capture".

New:
- An app icon.
- History. Every puzzle is saved as you photograph it, listed under day headings,
  and reopens exactly as you left it. Delete any of them.
- Settings, About and the legal notices, including open source attribution.
- Auto-capture can be switched off.

On privacy: the app has no internet permission at all - a dependency was quietly
adding a network permission and it has been removed, so the permission list is now
just the camera. Photos never leave the phone.
