Fixes from the first run on a phone.

The big one: when a cell is marked red, the app now draws the digit IT read on top.
Before, you saw the correct digit on your paper tinted red and reasonably concluded
the solver was broken. It was not - recognition had misread that cell, and the
overlay gave you no way to tell. Now a misread looks like a misread. Tap it to fix.

The same misreading explained three other reports: Hint and Solve pointing at cells
that already had a digit, and some written digits never being highlighted. Those
cells were read as empty, so they genuinely were the next thing to fill.

Also fixed:
- Solve now overlays the whole solution, not only the cells read as empty.
- Hint stops once the puzzle is complete.
- "Check the highlighted cells" clears once you have been through them, and each
  cell now has a "Looks right" button so you can confirm a reading without editing it.
- The viewfinder is square, and the shutter no longer sits under the system buttons.
- Shutter says "Capture".

Not fixed yet, and honestly:
- The heavily annotated puzzle that failed. I tried an adaptive rule for telling
  candidate marks from digits and it made things worse on photos that already
  worked, so I reverted it. I need that photo to fix it properly rather than guess.
- No app icon, settings, about or history yet.
